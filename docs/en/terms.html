Termeh Piano Terms of Use

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026

These terms explain the Termeh Piano educational app and its optional advanced lessons. Applicable consumer rights and Cafe Bazaar rules remain in force.

1. Use by children

Termeh Piano introduces basic music reading and rhythm through play. Parents and guardians decide when children use the app, manage devices and authorize purchases. The app is not a substitute for a qualified music teacher.

2. Free lessons

The app is free to download, and the first five starter treble-clef notes are available without payment. Subsequent learning stages unlock through practice and assessment.

3. Optional in-app purchase

Advanced lessons, bass clef and note durations are offered through one optional, non-consumable lifetime purchase in Cafe Bazaar, not a subscription. The product's configured price is currently 99,000 toman; only the live official Bazaar checkout displays the final payable amount. A parent must separately confirm in the parent section and in Bazaar's checkout. Purchase establishes ownership of premium lessons, while bass clef and note durations become playable only after completing the treble-clef learning sequence and final assessment. A learning prerequisite is not a failed purchase.

4. Offline access and restoration

Core lessons work offline. The initial purchase and restoration after reinstall or changing phones require an internet connection and a Cafe Bazaar account. The encrypted advanced lessons are bundled in the app, not separately downloaded, and become playable after verified ownership. The child's saved learning progress stays on the device and is not synchronized across devices by purchase restoration.

Optional daily reminder

After answering the parent math gate, a parent may choose to turn on the Android daily reminder, grant notification permission, or turn it off at any time. Reminders are scheduled for approximately 6 PM local device time but may be delayed by battery optimization and are skipped after a completed exercise that day. Only the reminder preference and day-level practice timestamp are kept on the device. No push service or child account is required.

5. Data and privacy

Learning progress is currently stored on the device and can be lost when uninstalling or clearing app data. Read our Privacy Policy. Error reports are not automatically emailed.

6. Updates and support

Updates may be required for security or compatibility. Mandatory updates are governed only by the current published release policy. Parents can access technical support and error-reporting options in the app's parent section.

7. Educational scope and no guaranteed outcome

Termeh Piano is a supplementary educational game for basic music-note reading. It is not the primary source of music education or a replacement for a qualified music teacher and hands-on practice with an instrument. Learning outcomes vary by the child's age, readiness, experience and practice. No particular skill level or piano-playing proficiency is guaranteed. Accuracy and speed are in-game performance measures only.

Some activities are informed by research on spaced review, active retrieval and feedback. The effectiveness of Termeh Piano itself has not been established by independent scientific research, and the referenced organizations have not endorsed or certified this app. This statement does not restrict statutory consumer rights.

8. Limitations and changes

The app may have limitations or defects. Purchases are possible only when the official Bazaar product is available and a parent authorizes checkout. The Bazaar checkout shows the final price and terms. Applicable consumer protections and Cafe Bazaar policies continue to apply.
