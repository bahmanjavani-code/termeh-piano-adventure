Termeh Piano Terms of Use

Last updated: October 25, 2026

These terms help parents understand the Termeh Piano beta. They may be updated before commercial publication to comply with Cafe Bazaar's requirements.

1. Use by children

Termeh Piano introduces basic music reading and rhythm through play. Parents and guardians decide when children use the app, manage devices and authorize purchases. The app is not a substitute for a qualified music teacher.

2. Free lessons

The commercial app is planned as a free download. Starter treble-clef lessons will be free. The beta may additionally include advanced preview material for testing only.

3. Optional in-app purchase

We plan to offer permanent advanced lessons, bass clef and rhythm through a single lifetime in-app purchase. The proposed price is 99,000 toman; the final price and purchase terms will appear in Cafe Bazaar's official checkout. Payments are disabled in the beta and no money is collected. When enabled, purchases will require parent action and store confirmation. This is not a recurring subscription.

4. Offline access and restoration

Core lessons work offline. Buying premium lessons, downloading protected content, and restoring an earlier purchase after reinstalling or changing phones will require an internet connection and a Cafe Bazaar account. The precise restore policy will be published before sales begin.

5. Data and privacy

Learning progress is currently stored on the device and can be lost when uninstalling or clearing app data. Read our Privacy Policy. Error reports are not automatically emailed.

6. Updates and support

Updates may be required for security or compatibility. Mandatory updates are disabled in the beta. Parents can access technical support and error-reporting options in the app's parent section.

7. Educational scope and no guaranteed outcome

Termeh Piano is a supplementary educational game for basic music-note reading. It is not the primary source of music education or a replacement for a qualified music teacher and hands-on practice with an instrument. Learning outcomes vary by the child's age, readiness, experience and practice. No particular skill level or piano-playing proficiency is guaranteed. Accuracy and speed are in-game performance measures only.

Some activities are informed by research on spaced review, active retrieval and feedback. The effectiveness of Termeh Piano itself has not been established by independent scientific research, and the referenced organizations have not endorsed or certified this app. This statement does not restrict statutory consumer rights.

8. Limitations and changes

The beta may contain bugs and advertised future features are not yet active. Terms and purchase details will be revised before monetization. Applicable consumer protections and Cafe Bazaar policies continue to apply.
