Release status

Public release of version 1.0.2 is pending final approval. An actual Cafe Bazaar lifetime purchase and reinstall restoration have been verified on a real phone; the app is not yet publicly distributed. No online child accounts or cloud progress backups are offered. This website does not sell anything; optional lifetime purchases, once released, will be made only inside the app through official Bazaar checkout.

Privacy and support

Music practice runs locally and progress is currently stored on the device. The app does not use the microphone, camera, or location for lessons. Internet connectivity may be used to check for app updates.

Parents may open a draft error report in their email app; reports are not sent automatically. A public support email will be announced once the support mailbox is configured.
