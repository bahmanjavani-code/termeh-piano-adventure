Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the Termeh Piano Android app and product website. Five starter notes are free; optional advanced lessons can be unlocked by a one-time lifetime purchase through Cafe Bazaar with parent confirmation. No online child account is created.

1. Learning data

Game progress, practice answers, and in-app preferences are stored locally on the device. Online accounts and cloud synchronization are not enabled in the current beta.

2. Sensitive permissions

The app does not request microphone, camera, or location permissions for music lessons.

Optional on-device practice reminders

Only after a parent opts in through the protected parent section, Android notifications may be used for a daily practice reminder (POST_NOTIFICATIONS runtime permission on Android 13+). The enable/disable choice, the last completed practice date, and the last reminder date are kept on this device. No child's name, answer or score is added to reminders or sent to a server. Parents can turn reminders off at any time. Android battery policies may change the scheduled delivery time.

3. Internet and updates

Core lessons work offline. To check for newer versions, the app may contact a version policy file hosted by GitHub. As with any internet request, the network IP address may be visible to the service provider. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

4. Error reports

Limited technical details, such as error category, source line number, app version, and Android version, may be stored on the device. A parent can review and choose to send an error report or suggestion through their email app. Parents can inspect and edit any message before sending. Automatic upload of error reports is not enabled.

5. Payments and accounts

The app does not provide an online child account or cloud progress backup. An optional lifetime upgrade is purchased through Cafe Bazaar's official checkout. The app does not ask for or store card numbers, banking passwords, or payment credentials. The Cafe Bazaar SDK supplies purchase ownership and technical entitlement data that the app verifies; a valid entitlement is stored in protected local device storage. Cafe Bazaar may process transaction data under its own privacy policy. Restore after reinstall or changing devices requires the same Bazaar account and an internet connection; purchase restoration does not restore saved lesson progress.

6. Retention and deletion

Learning progress remains in local app storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may erase it; cloud restoration is not currently available.

7. Contact

Support contact details are listed in the app's Cafe Bazaar listing and in its parent section. Please do not submit children's personal information, bank card information, or purchase tokens in diagnostic reports.
