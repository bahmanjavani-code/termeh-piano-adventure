🎹 ماهیت و هدف آموزشی برنامهPurpose and educational scope

The free edition contains five starter treble notes. Once the store upgrade is enabled, advanced treble, bass and rhythm lessons require an official one-time Bazaar purchase as well as normal learning progress.
